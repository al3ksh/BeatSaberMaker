# 2026-11-13 — first VR play-test

Played in a headset (Beat Saber). Checkpoints: timing step 32000, pattern step 28000. Maps as generated on 2026-11-12 after the intro trim, all `--seed 0`.

User's notes (Alex, translated from Polish):

- Feel in the headset: "very good".
- `BSM - Camellia - GHOST (from Cyphisonia E.P.) [nps 6, unknown]`: notes spawned too close, which made the map unplayable.

Cause of the GHOST problem (measured): the start-beat offset was taken from dataset statistics in beats, and the grid detection reported 110 BPM for a 220 BPM song, so the reaction time came out at 355 ms. Human maps at this density have a median of 580 ms (10th percentile 500 ms). The other maps had 600-940 ms.

Fix (branch etap2-spawn): the offset is now solved from a target reaction time in seconds per density bin (human medians), independent of the detected BPM. GHOST is now at 580 ms; maps at nps 4 moved from 750-870 ms to 714 ms, nps 3 from 938 to 863 ms.

This closes the Stage 1 criterion "play at least 3 generated maps in VR".
