## Follow-up on "illogical" moves and the quiet intro (measured, not user feedback)

- Downward cuts in the top row are not over-produced: 0.51% of generated notes versus 0.68% in human maps (40 val songs, pattern model on human event times), and in both they mostly follow an upward cut of the same hand (85% / 89%). No mask rule added.
- What is over-produced: upward cuts in the bottom row, 20.5% of notes versus 10.8% in human maps, mostly straight after a downward cut; middle-row diagonals are about half the human share.
- Stray lead-in (at most 8 events, then a gap of 6 s or more within the first 30 s): 6.2% of generated songs versus 1.9% of human maps. Such groups are now dropped; FIRESTARTER starts at 0:13.0 again.
